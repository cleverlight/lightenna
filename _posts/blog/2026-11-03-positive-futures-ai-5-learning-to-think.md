This is a grammar school presentation for sixth-formers.
Their school, rightly, asks them not to use AI for their studies: their work must be their own.
The workplace they're heading into will expect them to use AI expertly from their first day.
This talk is about bridging those two worlds, and why the first one prepares them for the second.
